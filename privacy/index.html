Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Rilo Food ("Rilo", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you use the Rilo Food mobile application, website, and related services (the "Services").

Rilo Food is an AI meal planning app that helps users create meal plans, recipes, and shopping lists based on selected days, meal slots, budget, retailer, servings, diet and allergy notes, pantry items, cooking preferences, and food goals.

Summary

  • Rilo Food does not currently require a sign-up account.
  • The app may use an anonymous local profile ID to keep meal plans, pantry items, shopping-list state, and subscription access working.
  • We do not collect payment-card details. App Store purchases are processed by Apple, and subscription entitlements are managed through RevenueCat.
  • We do not sell personal information, share personal information with data brokers, or use third-party advertising SDKs.
  • We do not intentionally collect precise location, contact lists, browsing history, or search history.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the Services, we may collect or process the following information.

Information You Provide

  • Meal planning preferences, including selected days, meal slots, budget, retailer, servings, diet notes, allergy notes, pantry items, cooking preferences, cooking equipment notes, food goals, and similar planning inputs.
  • User-generated content in the app, including generated meal plans, recipes, ingredients, method text, shopping lists, checked items, owned items, meal swaps, locked meals, completed meals, skipped meals, and regeneration history.
  • Support or privacy request information you choose to provide through the support page or other contact method.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Anonymous app profile identifiers used to provide app functionality without requiring a sign-up account.
  • Device and usage data, such as app interactions, diagnostics, logs, feature use, request timing, and technical information needed to operate and protect the Services.
  • Subscription status, offering, purchase, renewal, and entitlement information needed to provide Rilo Pro access.

Sensitive Information

Diet notes, allergy notes, health-related goals, or similar free-text meal planning inputs may reveal health-related information if you choose to enter them. Please only enter information that you are comfortable using for meal planning. Rilo Food is not a medical, nutrition, allergy, or clinical advice service.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Generate, revise, store, and display meal plans, recipes, pantry-aware suggestions, and shopping lists.
  • Personalise meal planning around your selected budget, retailer, servings, preferences, diet notes, allergy notes, pantry items, and goals.
  • Maintain app state, including plans, pantry items, shopping-list progress, meal locks, swaps, skipped meals, completed meals, and anonymous profile continuity.
  • Provide, manage, restore, and verify subscription access and Rilo Pro entitlements.
  • Operate, debug, secure, monitor, and improve the Services.
  • Respond to support, privacy, deletion, correction, or export requests.
  • Comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

AI Services

Rilo Food uses AI services to generate and revise meal plans, recipes, shopping lists, and related app content. Meal planning inputs may be sent to Google Cloud AI or Google Gemini through server-side systems to provide these features. AI-generated content may be incomplete, inaccurate, or unsuitable for your needs. You should review ingredients, allergens, nutrition estimates, product labels, prices, and cooking instructions before relying on a plan.

Service Providers

We may disclose information to service providers that help us operate the Services. These providers process information on our behalf or as otherwise described in their own privacy notices.

  • Supabase: backend hosting, database, user data storage, and server-side functions.
  • Google Cloud AI / Google Gemini: AI meal planning, recipe, and shopping-list generation.
  • RevenueCat: in-app purchase management, subscription status, and entitlement processing.
  • Apple App Store: app distribution, in-app purchase processing, subscriptions, refunds, and related App Store services.

We do not store payment-card details in Rilo Food. Payments and subscriptions are processed through Apple App Store, and subscription entitlement information is managed by RevenueCat.

When We Disclose Information

We may disclose information:

  • To service providers listed above so they can provide hosting, database, AI, purchase, subscription, security, and operational services.
  • To comply with law, legal process, regulatory requests, or enforceable governmental requests.
  • To protect the rights, safety, security, and integrity of users, Rilo Food, our Services, or others.
  • In connection with a business transfer, such as a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganisation, or sale of assets.

Tracking, Advertising, and Sale of Information

Rilo Food does not currently use third-party advertising SDKs, sell personal information, share personal information with data brokers, or track users across other companies' apps or websites for targeted advertising. We do not currently use Google Analytics or Google Maps APIs in the Services.

Retention

We keep information for as long as necessary to provide the Services, maintain meal planning history and subscription entitlements, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information, whether the anonymous app profile remains active, operational needs, legal requirements, and user requests.

Security

We use technical and organisational safeguards designed to protect information. However, no method of transmission, storage, or processing is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

International Transfers

We and our service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. These countries may have data protection laws that differ from those in your location. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards or legal bases for international transfers.
