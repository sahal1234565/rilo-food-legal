Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Rilo Food ("Rilo Food", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, discloses, stores, and protects information when you use the Rilo Food mobile application, website, and related services (the "Services").

Rilo Food is an AI meal planning app for users in the United Kingdom. It helps users create meal plans, recipes, and shopping lists based on selected days, meal slots, budget, retailer, servings, diet and allergy notes, pantry items, cooking preferences, and food goals.

Summary

  • Rilo Food does not currently require a sign-up account.
  • The app uses an anonymous local profile ID so meal plans, pantry items, shopping-list state, and subscription access can work without an account.
  • We do not collect payment-card details. App Store purchases are processed by Apple, and subscription entitlements are managed through RevenueCat.
  • Diet, allergy, and health-related planning notes may reveal sensitive health-related information if you choose to enter them.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not intentionally collect precise location, contact lists, browsing history, or search history.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the Services, we may collect or process the following information.

Information You Provide

  • Meal planning preferences, including selected days, meal slots, budget, retailer, servings, diet notes, allergy notes, pantry items, cooking preferences, equipment notes, food goals, and similar planning inputs.
  • User-generated app content, including generated meal plans, recipes, ingredients, method text, shopping lists, checked items, owned items, meal swaps, locked meals, completed meals, skipped meals, and regeneration history.
  • Support or privacy request information you choose to provide when contacting us.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Anonymous app profile identifiers used to provide app functionality without requiring a sign-up account.
  • Device and usage data, such as app interactions, diagnostics, logs, feature use, request timing, and technical information needed to operate, troubleshoot, and protect the Services.
  • Subscription status, offering, purchase, renewal, and entitlement information needed to provide Rilo Pro access.

Sensitive Information

Diet notes, allergy notes, health-related goals, or similar free-text meal planning inputs may reveal health-related information if you choose to enter them. Please only enter information that you are comfortable using for meal planning. Rilo Food is not a medical, nutrition, allergy, dietetic, or clinical advice service.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Generate, revise, store, and display meal plans, recipes, pantry-aware suggestions, and shopping lists.
  • Personalise meal planning around your selected budget, retailer, servings, preferences, diet notes, allergy notes, pantry items, and goals.
  • Maintain app state, including plans, pantry items, shopping-list progress, meal locks, swaps, skipped meals, completed meals, and anonymous profile continuity.
  • Provide, manage, restore, and verify subscription access and Rilo Pro entitlements.
  • Operate, debug, secure, monitor, and improve the Services.
  • Identify usage trends and protect the Services from misuse, abuse, or technical issues.
  • Respond to support, privacy, deletion, correction, access, or export requests.
  • Comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Legal Bases for UK and European Users

Where UK, European Economic Area, or Swiss data protection laws apply, our legal bases may include performance of a contract, legitimate interests, consent, and compliance with legal obligations. Our legitimate interests include providing, securing, improving, and protecting the Services. If you provide health-related or allergy-related meal planning information, we process it only as needed to provide the requested meal planning features or where you have chosen to provide it.

AI Services

Rilo Food uses AI services to generate and revise meal plans, recipes, shopping lists, and related app content. Meal planning inputs may be sent to Google Cloud AI through server-side systems to provide these features. AI-generated content may be incomplete, inaccurate, or unsuitable for your needs. You should review ingredients, allergens, nutrition estimates, product labels, prices, and cooking instructions before relying on a plan.

You can contact us using the contact details below if you have questions about our use of AI services.

We may disclose information to service providers and business partners that help us operate, improve, measure, or promote the Services. These providers process information on our behalf or as otherwise described in their own privacy notices.
