Privacy Policy

Rilo helps users create meal plans and shopping lists from selected days, meal slots, budget, retailer, servings, diet and allergy notes, pantry items, cooking preferences, and food goals.

Information Rilo Collects

Rilo may collect or store the following information to provide app features:

  • Anonymous app user ID generated by the app.
  • Meal-planning inputs, including selected dates, meal slots, servings, budget, retailer, goals, diet notes, allergy notes, pantry notes, pantry items, and cooking or equipment notes.
  • Generated meal-plan content, recipe details, ingredients, method text, estimated cost, and nutrition estimates.
  • Shopping-list state, including checked items, owned items, removed review items, and source meal references.
  • Meal interaction state, including locked meals, completed meals, skipped meals, swaps, scope edits, and regeneration history.
  • Profile defaults, including default retailer, budget, servings, diet notes, allergy notes, and cooking notes.
  • Subscription status and purchase information needed to provide Rilo Pro access.

Rilo does not intentionally collect contact lists, precise location, browsing history, search history, or payment-card details.

How Rilo Uses Information

Rilo uses information to generate meal plans and shopping lists, preserve plans and pantry items, support revisions and swaps, personalize planning around user preferences, provide and restore Rilo Pro access, diagnose app or backend issues, and protect the integrity of the service.

Service Providers

Rilo uses service providers to operate the app:

  • Supabase stores anonymous profile data, plans, pantry items, shopping state, and revision history.
  • Google Gemini is called server-side through Supabase Edge Functions to generate and revise meal plans.
  • RevenueCat manages subscription offerings, purchase status, entitlement access, and restore flows.
  • Apple processes App Store purchases and subscriptions.

Private API keys are stored server-side and are not embedded in the iOS app.

AI-Generated Content

Meal plans and recipes are generated with AI and server-side checks. Users should review ingredients, allergens, nutrition estimates, product labels, prices, and cooking instructions before relying on a plan. Rilo is not medical advice.

Tracking and Advertising

Rilo does not currently use third-party advertising SDKs, sell personal information, share user data with data brokers, or track users across other companies' apps or websites for advertising purposes.

Anonymous Profile and Future Account Linking

The app currently uses an anonymous local profile ID. If sign-in is added later, Rilo may offer a way to link a sign-in identity to the existing anonymous profile so plans, pantry items, shopping state, and subscription identity can be preserved.

Data Retention

Rilo keeps anonymous profile data while the app profile remains active and the service needs it to provide meal planning, shopping list, pantry, and subscription functionality.

User Choices

Users can avoid entering optional free-text notes, edit profile defaults in the app, remove shopping review items from the active list, stop Rilo Pro through Apple subscription settings, and request support, deletion, or export through the support page.

Children

Rilo is not designed for children and should not be directed to children without parent or guardian involvement.

Changes

Rilo may update this Privacy Policy as the app changes. The published policy will show the current effective date.

Contact

For support, privacy, deletion, or export requests, use the public support page at Rilo Support. Do not post sensitive health, billing, or personal information in public GitHub issues.
