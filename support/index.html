Rilo Support

Rilo helps create flexible meal plans and shopping lists from your selected days, meal slots, budget, retailer, pantry items, preferences, and goals.

Getting Started

  1. Open Rilo.
  2. Tap get started.
  3. Choose 1 to 7 days or specific dates.
  4. Select breakfast, lunch, dinner, snacks, or any combination.
  5. Add servings, budget, retailer, goals, diet or allergy notes, pantry notes, and cooking preferences.
  6. Generate your plan.

Using Rilo

After a plan is created, use Today for the current day's meals, Plan for the full meal plan, Shop for the generated shopping list, Pantry for saved items, and Profile for defaults, anonymous profile ID, purchases, and restore.

Rilo Pro

Rilo Pro unlocks advanced plan revisions, including scope edits, meal swaps, full plan regeneration, and richer shopping carryover after revisions.

To restore purchases, open Profile, tap restore, and wait for subscription status to refresh. Apple handles subscription billing, and users can manage or cancel subscriptions through Apple ID subscription settings.

Anonymous Profile

Rilo does not require login in the current release. The app creates a local anonymous profile ID so plans and purchases can work without an account.

Food Safety

Rilo uses AI-generated meal ideas. Always check ingredients, allergens, product labels, nutrition estimates, cooking instructions, and suitability for your own dietary or medical needs. Rilo is not medical advice.

Troubleshooting

If plan generation fails

  • Check your network connection.
  • Try a simpler or smaller plan.
  • Increase the budget.
  • Remove one soft goal.
  • Add simpler cooking notes.

If subscription products do not load

  • Check your network connection.
  • Open Profile and refresh.
  • Confirm App Store purchases are available on the device.

If restore does not find a purchase

  • Confirm you are using the Apple ID that bought the subscription.
  • Confirm the subscription is still active.
  • Try again after a few minutes.
